1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Frill Monarch (“we”, “us”), of 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, collects and uses personal information when you visit frillmonarch.com, send an order request, join our newsletter or contact our studio. We collect only what we need to run the shop and look after you as a customer, and we never sell your information. Questions are welcome at hello@frillmonarch.com or +1-888-777-5845.

2. What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, the pieces, sizes and colors you choose, and any notes such as a gift message.
  • Messages: anything you send through our contact form or by email, including an order reference if you add one.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Payment: payments are completed through our payment provider's secure page. We receive confirmation of payment but never see or store your full card details.
  • Browsing data: device, browser, pages viewed and approximate location, collected through cookies only if you allow them. See our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use it

We use your information to confirm stock, send you a payment link, pack and ship your order, handle returns and exchanges, answer questions and keep accurate business records. If you subscribe, we send one newsletter a month with new pieces and styling notes; every email has an unsubscribe link. With your consent, we also use aggregated analytics to understand which pages and pieces are useful so we can improve the shop.

4. Bag, wishlist and saved orders on your device

Your shopping bag, wishlist and a copy of any order request you send are stored in your own browser's local storage so they're still there when you come back. We cannot see this information, and it stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for frillmonarch.com.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the shop: our payment provider, shipping carriers, our email and hosting providers, and — with your consent — analytics and advertising partners. Each is allowed to use your information only to provide their service to us. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to prevent fraud.

6. Google Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics and may use Google advertising services, only after you choose “Accept all” in our cookie banner. We use Google Consent Mode so these tools stay in a limited, cookieless state until then. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalization in your Google account.

7. How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for seven years to meet tax and accounting requirements. Messages are kept for up to two years. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. Order requests that are never completed are deleted after 12 months. After that, information is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Keeping it safe

Our site uses encrypted HTTPS connections, access to customer information is limited to the people who need it, and we choose providers with strong security practices. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will tell you and the relevant authorities about a breach where the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, get a copy of it, object to certain uses and opt out of targeted advertising. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete and correct, and not to be treated differently for using them. Email hello@frillmonarch.com and we'll respond within the time the law allows.

10. Children

Our shop is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe we have, please contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as our shop or the law changes. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date at the top.
