1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that websites save on your device. Local storage is a similar browser feature. Together they let a site remember things — like the pieces in your bag — between pages and visits. This policy explains exactly what frillmonarch.com stores, why, and how you can change your choices at any time.

4. Essential storage

Essential storage makes the shop work: without it your bag and wishlist would empty every time you changed page. It doesn't track you across other websites and can't be switched off in our banner, but you can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

5. Analytics and advertising

We use Google Consent Mode, so Google Analytics and advertising storage stay set to “denied” until you choose “Accept all”. Before then, Google may receive only limited, cookieless signals. If you accept, we can see which pieces and pages are most helpful and improve the shop. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

6. Images

Product and editorial photographs are loaded from the Pexels image service. Loading an image shares standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, with that service, as with any image on the web. Pexels doesn't set advertising cookies through these images.

7. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you view and delete cookies and site data in their privacy settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work. Clearing site data for frillmonarch.com will also empty your bag and wishlist.
