Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Our returns promise

We want you to love every Frill Monarch piece you keep. If something isn't quite right, you have 30 days from the date of delivery to return or exchange it. Returns are free for orders delivered within the US, and we aim to make the process as easy as ordering in the first place.

2. What can be returned

Pieces must be unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with all original tags attached, and returned in their original packaging where possible. Please try things on over underwear and avoid perfume, deodorant or makeup contact, as we can't accept items with marks or scents.

  • For hygiene reasons, earrings can only be returned if their protective seal is unbroken.
  • Pieces marked as final sale at the time of purchase can't be returned unless faulty.
  • Gift purchases can be exchanged or refunded as store credit to the recipient.

3. How to return

  1. Email hello@frillmonarch.com with your order reference and the pieces you'd like to return or exchange.
  2. We'll reply within one business day with a prepaid US return label.
  3. Pack the pieces securely, attach the label and drop the parcel at the carrier location shown on the label.
  4. Keep your drop-off receipt until your refund or exchange is confirmed.

4. Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Tell us when you request your return and we'll reserve the new piece for you while your return travels back. If it's in stock, your exchange ships as soon as we scan your return — often before it reaches the studio. There's no extra shipping charge for exchanges within the US.

5. Refunds

Once your return arrives, we inspect it within two business days and email you to confirm. Refunds go back to your original payment method, and most card providers show the credit within 5–10 business days. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was faulty or we made a mistake with your order.

6. Faulty or incorrect items

If a piece arrives with a fault, or isn't what you ordered, please email us within 30 days with a photo. We'll arrange a free return and send a replacement or give a full refund, including any shipping you paid. Your statutory rights for faulty goods are not affected by this policy.

7. Returns after 30 days

We're unable to accept returns after 30 days, except for faulty items. If you're close to the deadline and have a genuine reason, get in touch anyway — we'll always look at it fairly.

8. Questions

For help with a return or exchange, email hello@frillmonarch.com or call +1-888-777-5845 on weekdays, 9:00 am – 6:00 pm ET.
