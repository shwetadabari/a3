Romance, with everyday ease

Frill Monarch is a small New York womenswear label making pretty, practical clothes — the kind you wear often and keep for years.

Where it began

A wardrobe that felt like us

Frill Monarch started with a simple frustration. Romantic clothes always seemed to belong to special occasions: too delicate to wash, too fussy to wear to work, too precious to sit down in. Everyday clothes, meanwhile, felt practical but a little joyless.

We wanted both. So we began designing pieces with the details we love — a soft ruffle, a pearl button, a skirt that swings — in fabrics that can handle real life. Linen that softens with every wash. Organic cotton poplin that holds a crisp collar. Silk that you're not afraid to wear.

The name came from a butterfly: the monarch, delicate to look at but built for long journeys. It felt like the right idea for clothes that are pretty and also made to go the distance.

What we believe

Three things we won't compromise on

01

Wearability

Every design is tested in daily life. If it can't survive a commute, a washing machine and a long lunch, it doesn't make the collection.

02

Honest materials

We list fiber content and care clearly on every piece, favor natural and recycled fabrics, and avoid finishes that wear off after a few washes.

03

Fewer, better

Small collections, careful restocks and timeless shapes mean less waste — and a wardrobe that works together.

Our studio

Designed in New York

Our small studio on Mercer Street is where ideas become samples, samples get worn and washed and worn again, and the final pieces are chosen. We work with experienced makers who share our standards, and we visit and review every production run before it reaches you.

Because we're small, you'll deal with real people. When you email the studio, the person who answers knows how each piece fits, drapes and washes — and will tell you honestly whether it's right for you.

Come and say hello at 181 Mercer Street, New York, or call us on +1-888-777-5845.

Caring for your pieces

The kindest thing you can do for your clothes — and the planet — is wear them often and care for them gently. Wash less and at lower temperatures, air-dry instead of tumble-drying, and steam rather than iron delicate fabrics. Each garment's label carries specific care instructions, and our studio team is always happy to advise on stains, storage or repairs.

Our promise to you

If something isn't right, tell us. We offer free returns and exchanges within 30 days in the US, and we read every message. Your feedback shapes the next collection.
