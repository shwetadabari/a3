How to measure

  • Bust: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: measure around your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the belly button.
  • Hip: stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

Fit notes, explained

Each product page tells you how a piece is designed to fit. True to size means choose your usual size. Relaxed or oversized pieces have extra room built in, so you might size down for a closer fit. Tailored pieces are cut closer to the body; if you're between sizes, or plan to layer knitwear underneath, choose the larger size.

Still unsure?

Our studio team is happy to help. Email hello@frillmonarch.com with your measurements and the piece you love, and we'll share the garment measurements and our honest advice. Exchanges for a different size are free within 30 days — see our Returns & Refund Policy.
