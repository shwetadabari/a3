Shipping Policy

1. Overview

We pack every Frill Monarch order by hand in our New York studio, wrapped in tissue and recyclable packaging. This policy explains our shipping options, costs and timings within the United States, and what to do if something goes wrong on the way to you. If you have a question that isn't answered here, please email hello@frillmonarch.com.

2. Shipping options and costs

MethodCostEstimated delivery after dispatch
Standard$8.95 · free on orders over $1503–6 business days
Express$18.001–2 business days

Delivery estimates are provided by our carriers and are not guaranteed, especially around public holidays or during severe weather.

3. When your order ships

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment being completed. Payments completed after 1:00 pm ET, or on weekends and US public holidays, begin processing the next business day. You'll receive an email with a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves the studio.

4. Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses within the 50 US states, including PO boxes for standard shipping. We are not yet able to ship internationally. If you'd like to know when we begin shipping to your country, email the studio and we'll let you know.

5. Checking your address

Please double-check your delivery address before completing payment. If you notice a mistake, contact us as quickly as possible; we can update the address if your order hasn't been dispatched. Once a parcel is with the carrier, we may not be able to change it.

6. Delayed, lost or damaged parcels

If your tracking hasn't updated for five business days, or your parcel is marked as delivered but you can't find it, please check with neighbors and your building's mail area first, then contact us. We'll open a claim with the carrier and, where a parcel is confirmed lost, send a replacement or issue a full refund. If your parcel arrives damaged, please photograph the packaging and the item and email us within 7 days.

7. Gifts

Add a gift message in the notes field when you send your order request, and we'll include it on a handwritten card. Prices are never included in the parcel when a gift message is added.

8. Questions

For anything shipping-related, email hello@frillmonarch.com or call +1-888-777-5845, Monday to Friday, 9:00 am – 6:00 pm ET. Please have your order reference to hand.
